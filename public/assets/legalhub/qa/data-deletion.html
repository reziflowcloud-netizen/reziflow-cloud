State the approximate test date and the identifier or account relationship needed to find the relevant record. Do not send credentials, tokens, App Secret, real client records or identity documents.

Manual handling

  1. The operator checks the request and verifies identity or authority using the least information necessary.
  2. The operator stops relevant QA intake and processing before cleanup. Stopping the relay or disconnecting the App alone does not erase data.
  3. The operator identifies the isolated QA dataset and related leads, receipts, dedupe records, assignments, notifications/outbox entries, logs, screenshots and operator-held QA database copies. These categories are checked separately.
  4. The operator manually removes relevant local records and copies and verifies the result. No production database or client organisation is included. No automatic erasure callback is provided by this page.
  5. The operator reports completion or explains any lawful restriction, remaining provider-held records and how to pursue the request with the provider.

Timing

For routine test closure, local QA dataset and diagnostic-copy cleanup is completed manually within 7 calendar days after closure or cancellation. Earlier pilot artifacts are reviewed and removed within 7 calendar days after the operator adopts this procedure. Local testing credentials are removed at closure, within 24 hours. Only a summary with no person/account/lead/device identifiers may remain for up to 30 calendar days after test closure and is then manually removed.

For an individual request, LegalHub acts without undue delay and responds within one month. Any permitted extension or justified refusal will be explained. A legal requirement to retain particular information is documented with its scope and period.

Meta and other provider records

The operator reviews the continued need for the QA App, Page and form at closure and within 7 days. Their administrator records remain on Meta while those resources exist. Removing a local QA record does not delete a Facebook account or erase all records held independently by Meta, Cloudflare or Vercel. Requests for those providers' own records follow their respective rights procedures, linked in the QA Privacy Policy.

Removing the App in Facebook settings revokes the relevant authorisation but does not replace the manual local deletion request. These are deletion instructions, not a URL for the signed-request callback protocol.
