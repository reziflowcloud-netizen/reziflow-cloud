Testing purpose and information

The purpose is to check whether one Meta test-form submission is delivered automatically to the isolated CRM, creates one lead, assigns it to a test staff account and creates an internal notification without duplicates. Contact fields use fabricated test names, email addresses and telephone numbers. Do not submit real contact, client, immigration, document or special-category information.

Technical information includes App, Page, form, test-lead and administrator/account identifiers, permission and access records, event timestamps, processing status, HTTP status and diagnostic error codes. Network providers also process connection information such as IP addresses. Administrator identifiers and technical metadata can be personal data even though the contact fields are synthetic. Private integration credentials are kept separately in operator-controlled server configuration, not in the public notice, support reports or contact records.

The source of Meta identifiers and event information is the owner's Meta resources and Graph API. The isolated CRM creates its own assignment, receipt and notification records. No lead or account data is used for marketing, sale, profiling or automated decisions affecting people.

Legal basis and access

For personal technical and administrator information, LegalHub relies on its legitimate interest under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR in verifying the reliability and security of its own integration. Access is restricted to the owner and specifically authorised internal QA operators. Only the owner's app-role account participates. Testing is not a requirement imposed on clients or employees, and no client data is needed. You may object using the contact below; LegalHub will assess the objection and restrict or stop the relevant testing while it is resolved.

Providers and locations

  • Meta: operates the Facebook App, Page, form, permissions and testing tools and supplies the test lead through Graph API. Its platform and account processing is described in Meta's Privacy Policy.
  • Operator-managed local CRM and PostgreSQL: process and store the test dataset on the operator's QA computer in Poland. The lead flow does not use Production CRM, Supabase or a customer database.
  • Cloudflare: provides the temporary public HTTPS relay for Meta webhook delivery. The relay carries event data and connection metadata to the local QA gateway. It is active only during the controlled test, not a permanent production endpoint. Cloudflare is a global provider, including processing in the United States and EEA; this is not an EEA-only route. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy and Data Processing Addendum.
  • Vercel: hosts these public static legal pages and processes page-request/network metadata. The existing hosting configuration includes the United States; it is not used to store the QA lead database. See Vercel's Privacy Notice and Data Processing Addendum.

LegalHub uses provider processing agreements and the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses for restricted international transfers for the hosting and HTTPS-relay services. Information about the applicable safeguards, including a copy subject to protection of confidential information, is available from the privacy contact. Meta's own platform transfers are described in its policy. No assertion is made that provider infrastructure or logs remain exclusively in Poland or the EEA.

This test uses CRM notifications inside the isolated application. It does not use external email, web push, document storage or production notification scheduling to deliver the QA lead notification. A request you send to the privacy contact is handled separately through the operator's business correspondence process; send only what is necessary.

Manual retention and shutdown

After the single controlled test closes or is cancelled, the operator closes the QA intake and worker and stops the temporary HTTPS relay. Disabling the test does not itself erase stored information.

Within 7 calendar days after closure or cancellation, the operator manually removes the isolated QA test dataset, including leads, receipts, deduplication records, staff-assignment and notification/outbox records, diagnostic files and screenshots, and operator-held QA database copies. Existing artifacts from earlier pilot runs are included in the review and removed within 7 calendar days after adoption of this procedure. This is a manual obligation, not an automatic deletion feature.

Only a result summary without person, account, lead, local user or device identifiers may be retained for up to 30 calendar days after test closure, then manually removed. The operator reviews continued need for the Meta QA App, Page and form at closure and within 7 days; platform administrator records persist while those resources exist. Local testing credentials are removed when the test closes, within 24 hours. Deleting or revoking Meta resources or authorisations is a separate owner action.

The operator checks for QA copies and verifies local deletion. This schedule does not promise erasure of Meta, Cloudflare or Vercel's independently retained platform/network records or business correspondence; provider retention and rights procedures apply to their own records. A legal obligation requiring retention is handled separately and explained to the requester, with the scope and applicable period identified.

Your rights and deletion requests

Where GDPR applies, you may request access, correction, erasure or restriction and object to legitimate-interest processing. Portability applies where its statutory conditions are met. You may complain to a supervisory authority, including UODO in Poland. There are no automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects in this pilot.

Email office@legalhubcrm.com with “LegalHub Internal QA privacy” and enough information to identify your request. Do not send passwords, access tokens, App Secret or identity documents. LegalHub will verify requests proportionately and respond within one month, subject to the GDPR rules for a justified extension. See the QA Data Deletion Instructions for the manual process.
