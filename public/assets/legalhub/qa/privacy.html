Testing purpose and information

The purpose is to check whether one Meta test-form submission is delivered automatically to the isolated CRM, creates one lead, assigns it to a test staff account and creates an internal notification without duplicates. Contact fields use fabricated test names, email addresses and telephone numbers. Do not submit real contact, client, immigration, document or special-category information.

Technical information includes App, Page, form, test-lead and administrator/account identifiers, permission and access records, event timestamps, processing status, HTTP status and diagnostic error codes. Network providers also process connection information such as IP addresses. Administrator identifiers and technical metadata can be personal data even though the contact fields are synthetic. Private integration credentials are kept separately in operator-controlled server configuration, not in the public notice, support reports or contact records.

The source of Meta identifiers and event information is the owner's Meta resources and Graph API. The isolated CRM creates its own assignment, receipt and notification records. No lead or account data is used for marketing, sale, profiling or automated decisions affecting people.

Legal basis and access

For personal technical and administrator information, LegalHub relies on its legitimate interest under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR in verifying the reliability and security of its own integration. Access is restricted to the owner and specifically authorised internal QA operators. Only the owner's app-role account participates. Testing is not a requirement imposed on clients or employees, and no client data is needed. You may object using the contact below; LegalHub will assess the objection and restrict or stop the relevant testing while it is resolved.

Manual retention and shutdown

After the single controlled test closes or is cancelled, the operator closes the QA intake and worker and stops the temporary HTTPS relay. Disabling the test does not itself erase stored information.

Within 7 calendar days after closure or cancellation, the operator manually removes the isolated QA test dataset, including leads, receipts, deduplication records, staff-assignment and notification/outbox records, diagnostic files and screenshots, and operator-held QA database copies. Existing artifacts from earlier pilot runs are included in the review and removed within 7 calendar days after adoption of this procedure. This is a manual obligation, not an automatic deletion feature.

Only a result summary without person, account, lead, local user or device identifiers may be retained for up to 30 calendar days after test closure, then manually removed. The operator reviews continued need for the Meta QA App, Page and form at closure and within 7 days; platform administrator records persist while those resources exist. Local testing credentials are removed when the test closes, within 24 hours; earlier local credential copies are removed within 24 hours of adoption of this procedure. The disposable local QA database and its private cluster copies are included in that early cleanup when they contain testing credentials. Deleting or revoking Meta resources or authorisations is a separate owner action.

The operator checks for QA copies and verifies local deletion. This schedule does not promise erasure of Meta, ngrok, Cloudflare or Vercel's independently retained platform/network records or business correspondence; provider retention and rights procedures apply to their own records. A legal obligation requiring retention is handled separately and explained to the requester, with the scope and applicable period identified.
