# Government data requests — proposed operating procedure

Prepared 17 November 2026. **OWNER APPROVAL AND ACTUAL ADOPTION REQUIRED. NOT AN EXISTING PRACTICE.** This draft does not change an answer in Meta or authorise a disclosure.

The owner confirmed on 17 November 2026 that the LegalHub operator has not disclosed personal data received from Meta in response to national-security government requests in the preceding twelve months. The owner also confirmed that none of Meta's listed government-request measures is currently implemented. The App Review draft for App `1627116975561049` therefore records **No disclosures** and **None of the above**, not the proposed measures below. ## Proposed responsibility and handling

Responsible decision maker: **VALENTYN VERBELCHUK**, operator of LegalHub CRM. Contact: **office@legalhubcrm.com**. A request must not itself grant access to a CRM account, token, backup or customer's records.

1. Receive and preserve the request privately. Verify the requesting authority, authenticity, jurisdiction, legal basis, scope and response deadline through an independently established official channel. Do not follow an unverified email link or provide credentials. Seek qualified legal advice when competence, scope, secrecy or cross-border authority is unclear.
2. Record the assessment and distinguish the controller. Where LegalHub acts as processor, notify and consult the relevant controller before responding, except where law prohibits it. Record any prohibition and its basis privately. Do not promise advance notice when forbidden.
3. Assess necessity, proportionality and valid grounds to challenge an unlawful or excessive demand. Obtain legal advice and pursue an available lawful challenge where grounds exist; this is not a promise to ignore a binding lawful order or to challenge every request irrespective of law.
4. Limit any legally required disclosure to the verified, necessary data and authorised recipient. An authorised operator prepares the minimum extract, checks its scope and transmits it through an approved secure channel. Do not provide wholesale access or unrelated tenant data.
5. Document the request, legal assessment, challenge decision, disclosure authorisation, precise disclosed categories and response. Restrict the record to authorised personnel. Reassess confidentiality restrictions and controller notification when permitted.

## Private register template

Use an access-restricted encrypted operational register, **not Git, this document or chat**. Proposed columns: internal case reference; received UTC; authority and verified contact; legal basis/jurisdiction; controller; scope and deadline; legality/proportionality assessment; adviser/decision maker; challenge decision and result; notification or prohibition basis; minimum categories disclosed; recipient/channel; response UTC; retention/legal-hold review date; closure authorisation. Do not duplicate the underlying client records merely for the register.

No register or disclosure-retention period is enacted by this draft. On actual adoption the owner must choose the protected register location, access and a documented review/retention criterion with legal advice as necessary. An outstanding lawful preservation obligation must be considered before erasure; it is not an indefinite blanket exception.
