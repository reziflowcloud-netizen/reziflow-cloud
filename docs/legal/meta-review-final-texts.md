### 8. Retention

Customer Leads, messages, cases and documents are retained for the customer’s instructed CRM purposes and service period, subject to verified deletion instructions and any applicable lawful retention requirement. Internal notifications, delivery receipts, diagnostic exports and backups are separate categories. Deleting a Lead or disabling an integration does not erase every related category. LegalHub does not represent that a universal automatic customer-data expiry schedule is installed.

The dedicated Lead Ads receipt-maintenance mechanism is manual and tenant-scoped. Terminal complete, blocked or shadow receipts older than 30 days become eligible for removal of direct lead references; a minimal keyed deduplication record can remain. Its removal becomes eligible after 366 days from the original receipt creation. Pending and processing receipts are excluded from that age-based cleanup. These are code-enforced eligibility limits, not a promise that a timer has already erased records on those dates, and the mechanism does not delete CRM Leads, notifications, logs or backups.

Technical provider logs and provider-held copies follow the applicable service configuration and contractual lifecycle; they are not erased by deleting a local CRM record. Operator diagnostic copies are retained only for the documented support/security purpose and reviewed on closure. Any justified retention exception and remaining copy categories are considered separately in responding to a deletion request.

For an isolated synthetic review session, closure is recorded when that session is stopped. The operator manually removes temporary local credential copies within 24 hours, review data and copies within 7 days, and the deidentified outcome report within 30 days after closure. This requires actual manual execution; it does not promise erasure of independent Meta or relay-provider records and does not reset earlier QA cleanup deadlines.

### 2. Disconnecting is separate from deletion

An authorised tenant administrator can disable the exact Lead Ads Page/form binding. The service operator manages its server-side token and, where appropriate, removes only the dedicated App’s relevant Page subscription or authorisation. Messenger and Instagram have separate controls and must not be disabled merely to disconnect Lead Ads.

Disconnection stops subsequent import for that binding; it does not automatically delete existing Leads, internal notifications, receipts, diagnostic copies or backups. OFF is not a retained queue and does not promise automatic backfill.

### 3. Scope of a deletion request

Available CRM deletion controls and operator-assisted procedures are different. Identify the organisation and records concerned, and where known the Page/form or CRM Lead reference. The controller assesses the request and lawful retention exceptions; LegalHub assists where it is processor. Where the interface lacks the required control, request service-level assistance instead of assuming the related data was erased.

- Consider the relevant Lead/client records, messages, notes, attachments and linked records within the authorised scope.
- Consider related internal notifications, direct lead references in delivery receipts, diagnostic exports and any operator-held copies separately.
- A minimal keyed deduplication record can be necessary and justified to prevent duplicate import. The adapter’s manual 30/366-day receipt eligibility rules are described in the Privacy Policy; they are not an automatic deletion of the Lead or all related records.
- Provider-held logs/backups and independent Meta records have separate controls and contractual or legal retention. A CRM deletion does not erase those copies immediately.

### 4. Response and remaining copies

Where GDPR applies, the controller responds without undue delay and normally within one month of receiving the request. A permitted extension of up to two further months requires notice and reasons within the first month. This is a response deadline, not a promise that every provider backup is physically erased immediately.

The response should distinguish deleted active records from any retained category, its justification and the applicable retention period or criteria. If erasure is refused or limited, the controller explains the reason and available complaint/remedy options. Restoring a backup does not justify resuming use of records subject to an applicable deletion decision; that decision must be reconciled before use.
