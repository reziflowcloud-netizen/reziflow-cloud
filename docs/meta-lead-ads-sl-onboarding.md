# First SL organization — activation gate

This package prepares a later owner decision. It does not authorize contacting SL, reading its leads, obtaining its tokens, subscribing a Page, expanding permissions or enabling any flag. Stage A own-role QA PASS is not proof of third-party access.

## Meta access evidence before onboarding

Choose an owner-controlled **production** Lead Ads App distinct from the internal QA resources and existing Messenger/Instagram apps. In that exact App's current dashboard record the approved use case, publisher/business owner, Review status, effective Standard/Advanced access for each required permission, Tech Provider eligibility and any Business Verification/Data Use Checkup requirements. For a third-party organization's Page, obtain Advanced Access/App Review where Meta requires it; Business Verification and Tech Provider requirements are dashboard-dependent and remain UNVERIFIED until that actual App is selected and inspected. Do not claim own-role “ready for testing” means third-party access is approved. Live Mode alone neither grants Advanced Access nor guarantees webhook delivery.

The [official retrieval guide](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/guides/lead-ads/retrieving/) currently lists leads_retrieval, pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, pages_manage_ads and ads_management for all lead/ad data; pages_manage_metadata is needed for webhook subscription. The runtime reads only id/form_id/platform/field_data/created_time and does not manage advertisements or subscriptions. Determine the exact minimum permitted token scopes/dependencies in the selected App validator and permission reference before requesting them; do not automatically grant ads_management/business_management or copy the QA five-scope result as an external entitlement. See [App Review](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/app-review/) and [access levels](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/graph-api/overview/access-levels/).

Verify SL's authorized representative may provide Page/Form and lead access. Confirm exact Page and published Form ownership, Lead Access Manager grants, token-issuing identity/role and granularity, separate App Page-leadgen subscription and Page subscribed_apps record. No Page names or browser profile names substitute for exact IDs/access evidence. No real ad or new token is needed to prepare this checklist.

## Legal and credential ownership

Before real contacts: SL approves its lead-form notice, lawful basis and controller identity; the CRM operator's processor/service role, Article28 DPA (the existing draft is not signed), subprocessors, actual storage regions/transfers/safeguards, retention for leads/receipts/tombstones/notifications/logs/backups, deletion/access/export/incident instructions. QA-only privacy pages cannot authorize SL data. Do not make unverified promises about backup erasure, automatic deletion or international safeguards. Controller/processor responsibility and operational incident contacts must be accepted by both parties.

Record privately the tenant ID and exact production App/Page/Form, SL authority and issuing account, server secret owner/access, token renewal/revocation contact and recovery process. Store Page tokens only in controlled server secrets; CRM UI/Organization JSON never receives them. Apply the release plan's private provenance/expiry/fingerprint procedure before an OFF binding is reserved. No self-service OAuth flow is implemented; manual onboarding must be operated by an approved administrator.

## Bounded first activation and rollback

1. Keep legacy Sheets/process running. Agree a time window, source-of-truth event ledger, responsibilities, authorized data minimization and exact no-loss/dedupe acceptance criteria. Native and Sheets pipelines currently have **different event keys**; cross-pipeline contact dedupe is not implemented. Do not run both as unrestricted lead creators. Keep native shadow OFF/ON only under approved shadow authority (no contact import), or explicitly suppress legacy creation for native-delivered event IDs through an approved process. Missing/mismatched IDs or inability to reconcile is an activation blocker, not a reason to dedupe merely by phone/email and merge different inquiries.
2. Configure Staff Routing only within SL: active Employee linked to active User, ordered channel members for Facebook/Instagram/unknown Lead Ads, fallback behavior, in-app notification preferences and push privacy policy. Verify no routing references another tenant. An unlinked fallback is intentionally left in administrator scope with a diagnostic.
3. First approve synthetic client submissions and exact pilot IDs. Check several distinct events, concurrent replay, different platform/unknown channels, assignment sequence, one internal notification per new lead and replay stability. Keep real contacts disabled until expected counts/timing and client permission gates pass. No paid ads are needed for this validation.
4. Authorize worker host/alerts and only SL UI/mode allowlists. Enable exact binding/global intake with fresh activation timestamps. Monitor Meta Track Status, receipt count/status/age and CRM assignment/notification counts; inspect contact data only under the later approved support purpose. Keep a reconciled gap ledger across OFF or token changes; webhook ACK while OFF is not storage/backfill.
5. SL-only STOP: binding DELETE -> confirm/readback -> remove SL mode/UI tuple/allowlists and token through controlled secret change -> reconcile gap with old process. Do not stop RAM/Messenger/Instagram/Sheets or other tenants. A global incident can stop the independent scheduler/global intake under separate incident scope. Preserve additive receipts/dedupe; do not reset attempts or delete history to force replay. Resume requires a new verified attestation/activation and explicit owner/client approval.

**Readiness:** onboarding checklist and rollback procedure prepared; actual third-party Review/Advanced/BV, signed client terms, authorized Page/Form/token and reconciliation process are still activation gates. No SL resource was inspected or changed during release preparation.
