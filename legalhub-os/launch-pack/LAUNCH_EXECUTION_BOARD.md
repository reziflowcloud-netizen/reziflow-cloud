### If Someone Asks About Price

```text
Good question. Final public pricing and plan terms need confirmation before we send details here.

To point you in the right direction, can you tell us:
1. how many people would use the CRM;
2. how many active cases you usually manage;
3. whether you mainly need leads, cases, documents, deadlines, payments, or owner visibility?

Then we can send the approved pricing or recommend the right next step once it is confirmed.
```

### If Someone Asks Why This Is Better Than Excel

```text
Excel is useful for storing rows.

The problem is that a legalization case needs more than a row: responsible person, next task, important dates, documents, comments, payment status, reminders, and status history.

LegalHub CRM is built to connect the work around the case, so the team can see what happened and what should happen next.

If you want, we can look at your current tracker and show what should move into a CRM workflow first.
```

### If Someone Asks Whether It Fits A Small Agency

```text
Yes, it can be relevant for small agencies if the work is already spread across Excel, WhatsApp, folders, calendars, and memory.

The useful question is not only team size. It is whether you need one place to see leads, clients, cases, documents, deadlines, payments, and responsibility.

How many people currently touch leads or cases in your agency?
```

### If Someone Wants To See The System

```text
Absolutely. The best first view is the lead-to-case workflow:

lead -> client -> case -> documents -> dates -> tasks -> payments -> responsible person.

Do you want to see the system from the owner view, lead follow-up view, or active case management view first?
```

If the link is ready:

```text
Here is the demo link: [insert approved booking link]
```

If not:

```text
Send us your email or phone number and we will send the next step manually.
```

### If The Person Is Not The Target Audience

```text
Thanks for the message. LegalHub CRM is built for legalization and migration agencies in Poland.

If you are looking for legal advice as an end client, this account is not the right place for case-specific guidance.
```
