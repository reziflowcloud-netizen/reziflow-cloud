```text
Open Your Spreadsheet. Now Show Me The Next Action.
```

Hook:

```text
Open your case spreadsheet. Now show me the next action.
```

Scene structure:

1. 0-3 sec: screen recording of fake spreadsheet. Cursor lands on one row.
2. 3-8 sec: voiceover: `Status says "in progress". But what happens next?`
3. 8-14 sec: zoom into missing fields: owner, next task, documents, comments, payments.
4. 14-25 sec: split screen: spreadsheet row vs LegalHub case record.
5. 25-33 sec: highlight LegalHub fields: status, responsible person, dates, documents, comments, payments, tasks.
6. 33-38 sec: end card with CTA.

## Short DM Response Script

Use as saved replies.

### DM: "Excel"

```text
Thanks. For the Excel workflow review, tell us:

1. Is your spreadsheet mainly for leads, clients, cases, payments, or deadlines?
2. How many people update it?
3. What is hardest to keep accurate?
4. Do you already have responsible people and next actions in the file?

We can then suggest what should move into a CRM workflow first.
```

### DM: "Leads"

```text
Thanks. For lead follow-up, quick check:

1. Where do leads come from today?
2. Who assigns them?
3. Do all leads have a status and next contact date?
4. What happens when a lead says yes?

If this is a current pain, we can show the lead-to-case workflow in a demo.
```

### DM: "Case"

```text
Thanks. A useful active case record should show owner, status, dates, documents, comments, payments, and next tasks.

Which of these is hardest for your team to see today?
```

### DM: "Demo"

```text
Great. LegalHub CRM demos work best when we start from your current workflow, not from a generic feature tour.

What should the demo focus on first: leads, cases, documents, deadlines, payments, or owner visibility?
```
