## Highlights Structure

Create these highlights before or during week 1.

### 1. Start Here

Purpose: explain who LegalHub CRM is for.

Frames:

1. CRM for legalization agencies in Poland.
2. For teams managing leads, clients, cases, documents, deadlines, payments, and responsibility.
3. Not legal advice for end clients. Not a generic CRM tips page.
4. CTA: book a workflow-based demo.

### 2. Demo

Purpose: show the product using approved demo data.

Frames:

1. Dashboard.
2. Leads.
3. Lead detail and next contact.
4. Client profile.
5. Case record.
6. Documents.
7. Tasks/calendar.
8. Payments/debt view.

### 3. Excel

Purpose: support Reel 02 and Carousel 01.

Frames:

1. Open your case spreadsheet.
2. Can it show the next action?
3. A row is not a case file.
4. What a case should show.
5. CTA: request a workflow audit.

### 4. Leads

Purpose: support Reel 04 and Day 4/8 stories.

Frames:

1. A lead without a next contact is already slipping away.
2. Track source, status, owner, and next contact.
3. Qualified leads should become client and case records.
4. CTA: book a lead workflow demo.

### 5. Cases

Purpose: support Carousel 02 and Reel 06.

Frames:

1. "In progress" is not enough.
2. Every active case should show owner, dates, documents, comments, payments, and tasks.
3. Show a labeled case detail screen.
4. CTA: book a case workflow demo.

### 6. Owners

Purpose: support Reel 05 and owner demo requests.

Frames:

1. The owner should not be the agency operating system.
2. Show active cases, upcoming work, debt/income, recent cases, responsibility.
3. Show dashboard/cases list with fake data.
4. CTA: request an owner visibility demo.

### 7. FAQ

Purpose: handle objections.

Frames:

1. We already use Excel.
2. Employees will not use CRM.
3. Our cases are too specific.
4. How is this different from a generic CRM?
5. What about privacy/security? Use only approved wording.
6. Which integrations are supported? Use setup caveats.

## Avatar Recommendation

Use:

- LegalHub CRM logo or simplified mark.
- High-contrast mark on clean background.
- No slogan inside the avatar.
- No CRM screenshot.
- No founder photo for the product account.

Minimum quality:

- centered icon or wordmark;
- clean edge spacing;
- not overloaded with text.
