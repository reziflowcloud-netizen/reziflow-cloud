## Lead Capture Principles

Use Instagram to start useful workflow conversations, not to force a hard sell.

Every lead-capture route should identify:

- whether the person works at a legalization/migration agency in Poland;
- which workflow is broken;
- whether the person has buying or operational influence;
- whether there is real urgency;
- whether demo, audit, or early access is the right next step.

Safe promise:

```text
We can show how LegalHub CRM supports your agency workflow from leads to clients, cases, documents, deadlines, payments, and responsibility.
```

Avoid:

- guaranteed outcomes;
- automatic migration;
- automatic WhatsApp import;
- official native integration claims;
- GDPR/compliance claims without approved wording;
- legally verified template claims;
- pricing/trial promises before approval.

## Capture Through DM

Use DM for:

- Story replies.
- Reel comments that ask for details.
- Keywords: `Excel`, `leads`, `case`, `deadlines`, `audit`, `demo`, `early access`.
- Warm founder/network conversations.

First DM:

```text
Thanks for reaching out. Quick question so we can point you to the right workflow:

Are you managing a legalization or migration agency in Poland, and are you currently tracking cases in Excel, WhatsApp, another CRM, or mixed tools?
```

Second DM after they answer:

```text
Which part breaks most often today?

1. lead follow-up
2. active case status
3. documents
4. deadlines/tasks
5. payments/debt
6. team responsibility
```

Demo handoff:

```text
This sounds like a good fit for a workflow-based demo.

Here is the booking link: [insert approved demo link]

For the demo, bring one example of how you track a lead, an active case, and a deadline today. Use fake or anonymized data only.
```

Audit handoff:

```text
This sounds like a good fit for a workflow audit.

We will look at where your current process breaks across leads, cases, documents, deadlines, payments, and responsibility.

Start here: [insert approved audit link]
```

If no link is ready:

```text
I can collect your details manually for now.

What is the best email or phone number for scheduling a workflow demo?
```

## Qualification Questions

Ask these before booking if the lead is cold:

1. Are you a legalization or migration agency in Poland?
2. What is your role: owner, operations manager, consultant, case manager, admin, other?
3. How many people work with leads or cases?
4. How many active cases do you usually manage?
5. What tools do you use now: Excel, WhatsApp, another CRM, folders, calendars, mixed tools?
6. Which workflow is most painful: leads, cases, documents, deadlines, payments, responsibility, owner visibility?
7. Are you looking for a CRM now, exploring for later, or just collecting ideas?
8. Do you want a demo, workflow audit, or early access information?

## Lead Qualification

### High Priority

Book a demo or audit quickly if:

- they are an owner, founder, operations lead, or decision influencer;
- they work with legalization/migration cases in Poland;
- they currently use Excel, WhatsApp, folders, or a generic CRM that does not fit case work;
- they mention missed follow-ups, unclear case status, documents, deadlines, unpaid balances, or owner visibility;
- they want to change process in the next 30-60 days.

### Medium Priority

Nurture and send relevant content if:

- they are a case manager or consultant without buying power;
- they have real pain but no immediate CRM decision;
- they are comparing tools;
- they ask practical implementation questions.

### Low Priority

Do not push sales if:

- they are an end client looking for legal advice;
- they are outside the target market;
- they only ask about immigration law;
- they ask for unsupported features such as client portal, AI/OCR, e-signature, government integrations, automatic billing, or automatic WhatsApp import.
