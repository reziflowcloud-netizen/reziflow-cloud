Last updated: 2026-07-08

## Planning Notes

Primary audience:

- Migration agency owners.
- Legalization consultants and lawyers.
- Case managers.
- Operations managers.

Primary narrative:

> LegalHub CRM helps legalization agencies move from Excel, WhatsApp, scattered folders, and memory into one structured workflow for leads, clients, cases, documents, deadlines, payments, and team responsibility.

Needs clarification before production:

- Final primary CTA: demo, workflow audit, registration, early access, or consultation.
- Approved demo screenshots and demo data.
- Approved pricing, proof points, testimonials, and security/privacy wording.
- Production-readiness of specific integrations.

## Weekly Rhythm

Week 1:

- Establish pain and category: LegalHub CRM is a specialized workflow CRM, not a generic sales CRM.

Week 2:

- Educate on documents, deadlines, dashboard, and lead follow-up.

Week 3:

- Handle objections: generic CRM, implementation, team adoption, migration, payment visibility.

Week 4:

- Increase product demos and direct CTAs for demo or workflow audit.
