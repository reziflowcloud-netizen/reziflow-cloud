LegalHub CRM is a specialized CRM and case-management system for legalization agencies in Poland. It supports the agency workflow from incoming requests and lead follow-up to client profiles, active cases, documents, reminders, payments, tasks, responsible employees, and dashboard visibility. Unlike generic CRMs that focus mainly on sales pipelines, LegalHub CRM is built around the daily operations of legalization work: complex client data, case statuses, important dates, documents, comments, and payment/debt tracking. It helps growing agencies move from Excel, WhatsApp, and scattered folders into one structured workspace.

### LegalHub CRM vs Excel

| Excel | LegalHub CRM |
| --- | --- |
| Stores rows and columns. | Connects leads, clients, cases, tasks, documents, payments, and responsibility. |
| Hard to enforce process consistency. | Uses statuses, assignments, reminders, and configurable settings. |
| Easy to duplicate or overwrite data. | Creates structured records with relationships between clients, cases, payments, and tasks. |
| Reports require manual work. | Dashboard and report pages surface key CRM data. |
| Does not manage follow-up by itself. | Supports next-contact reminders, tasks, calendar, and upcoming events. |

Excel is useful for lists, but legalization agencies need more than lists. LegalHub CRM helps turn spreadsheet data into a structured workflow for leads, clients, cases, documents, deadlines, payments, and team responsibility.

### LegalHub CRM vs WhatsApp

| WhatsApp | LegalHub CRM |
| --- | --- |
| Good for conversations. | Built for managing case workflow. |
| Information gets buried in chat history. | Keeps case data, documents, comments, dates, and tasks connected to records. |
| Responsibility is often informal. | Supports responsible people, assignments, tasks, and restricted access concepts. |
| Follow-ups depend on memory. | Supports reminders, due dates, calendar, and dashboard upcoming events. |
| Hard for managers to review. | Gives managers structured lists, dashboards, and status views. |

Message:

WhatsApp can support client communication, but it should not be the operating system of a legalization agency. LegalHub CRM gives the team a structured place to manage what has to happen next.
