Last updated: 2026-07-08

## Offer Strategy

LegalHub CRM should launch with offers that reduce three types of buyer risk:

1. Fit risk: "Will this match our legalization agency workflow?"
2. Adoption risk: "Will my team actually use it?"
3. Migration risk: "Can we move from Excel, WhatsApp, and folders without disruption?"

The first offers should be practical, workflow-based, and careful with technical claims.

## Offer 1: Free Demo

### Offer

Book a free LegalHub CRM demo for your legalization agency.

### Best For

- Agency owners.
- Operations managers.
- Small teams comparing CRM options.
- Agencies using Excel, WhatsApp, and shared folders today.

### Promise

See how LegalHub CRM can support the workflow from lead intake to client profile, active case, documents, deadlines, payments, tasks, and management visibility.

### What To Show

- Lead list, statuses, sources, reminders, and conversion.
- Client profile with case-relevant data.
- Case record with status, comments, documents, dates, payments, and tasks.
- Dashboard indicators for income, debt, clients, cases, and upcoming events.
- Settings for statuses, services, users, employees, fields, and templates.

### CTA

Book a free demo.

### Needs Clarification

- Approved demo environment and demo data.
- Approved screenshots or video assets.
- Whether demo should be self-serve, live, or both.

## Offer 2: Agency Workflow Audit

### Offer

Get a free audit of how your agency currently manages leads, cases, documents, deadlines, and payments.

### Best For

- Owners who feel operational pain but are not ready to buy CRM immediately.
- Agencies where the main pain is process chaos.
- Teams migrating from spreadsheets.

### Promise

We review the current workflow and identify where leads, documents, deadlines, responsibility, and payments are most likely to break down.

### Suggested Audit Questions

- Where do leads come from?
- How are leads assigned and followed up?
- When does a lead become a client?
- How are active cases tracked?
- Where are documents stored?
- How are deadlines and reminders managed?
- How are payments and debt tracked?
- Who can see which clients and cases?
- What reports does the owner need weekly?

### Output

- A short workflow map.
- Main operational gaps.
- Recommended CRM setup areas.
- Next-step recommendation: demo, migration plan, or early access.

### CTA

Request a workflow audit.

### Needs Clarification

- Whether this is delivered manually by sales/founder.
- Expected audit length and format.
- Whether audit is free for all leads or qualified leads only.

## Offer 5: Migration From Excel / WhatsApp

### Offer

Move your agency from Excel and WhatsApp into a structured CRM workflow.

### Best For

- Agencies with existing spreadsheets of clients, leads, or cases.
- Teams where WhatsApp contains too much operational history.
- Small agencies afraid of losing data during transition.

### Promise

We help you plan the move from spreadsheet-based tracking into LegalHub CRM records for leads, clients, cases, tasks, documents, and payments.

### Safe Product Basis

Supported by repository:

- CSV import.
- Import preview and mapping.
- Lead import with duplicate handling.
- Export flows.
- Unknown imported case columns can become custom fields.

Needs care:

- WhatsApp migration should be described as workflow migration unless there is a confirmed automated WhatsApp import.
- Do not promise automatic chat history import.

### CTA

Ask about migration from Excel.

### Needs Clarification

- Whether migration help is free, paid, or plan-specific.
- Whether the team provides hands-on data cleanup.
- Whether WhatsApp history can or should be imported manually.

## Recommended Offer Ladder

1. Content CTA: request workflow audit.
2. Sales CTA: book free demo.
3. Conversion CTA: guided onboarding or early access.
4. Expansion CTA: migration support and agency setup.

## Offer Guardrails

Do not promise:

- automatic WhatsApp import;
- automatic payment collection;
- automatic partner payouts;
- legally verified templates;
- guaranteed compliance;
- official Meta/Instagram/Google/Telegram setup without caveats;
- migration without customer effort;
- "done for you" onboarding unless service scope is approved.
