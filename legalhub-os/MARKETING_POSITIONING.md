## Main Positioning

LegalHub CRM is a specialized CRM and case-management workspace for legalization agencies in Poland that have outgrown spreadsheets, WhatsApp coordination, and generic sales CRMs.

It brings the agency workflow into one operational system: incoming requests, lead follow-up, client profiles, legalization cases, documents, deadlines, payments, tasks, responsible people, and management visibility.

The core promise is not "more contacts in a database." The promise is operational control over the full lead-to-case workflow of a legalization business.

## What Makes LegalHub Different From Ordinary CRMs

| Ordinary CRM | LegalHub CRM |
| --- | --- |
| Built mainly around generic sales pipelines and contact records. | Built around legalization agency operations: leads, clients, cases, documents, deadlines, payments, and responsible people. |
| Treats a client as a basic contact or account. | Supports structured client profiles with case-relevant immigration, passport, stay, address, family, travel, and custom data fields. |
| Requires heavy customization before it fits legalization workflows. | Starts from the workflow of legalization services in Poland and lets agencies configure statuses, services, sources, fields, and templates. |
| Often separates sales, documents, tasks, payments, and reporting into different tools. | Connects lead follow-up, case work, tasks, documents, payments/debt, and dashboard indicators in one CRM workspace. |
| Does not understand the difference between a sales deal and an active client case. | Supports lead conversion into client and case records, with case status history, comments, payments, dates, and documents. |
| Usually needs external spreadsheets for operational tracking. | Supports import/export while giving agencies a structured place to move spreadsheet-based work. |

## Who It Is Created For

LegalHub CRM is created for:

- Legalization agencies in Poland.
- Migration and immigration service companies helping foreigners with residence, work, PESEL, address registration, and related processes.
- Solo legalization specialists who need structure before chaos grows.
- Small teams that coordinate cases through chats, spreadsheets, folders, and memory.
- Growing agencies that need clear ownership, deadlines, payment visibility, and a repeatable operating process.
- Operations/admin managers who need to see who owns each lead or case and what needs attention next.

Assumption:

- The most likely primary buyer is the agency owner or operations lead, because the product includes organization settings, users, billing, dashboards, reports, and process configuration.

## Who It Is Not Created For

LegalHub CRM is not primarily created for:

- Generic sales teams that only need a simple contact pipeline.
- Enterprise legal departments that require custom enterprise procurement, complex legal matter management, or deep corporate compliance workflows.
- End clients/foreigners looking for a personal case portal. No end-client portal is confirmed in the current product map.
- Accounting-only teams that only need invoicing or bookkeeping.
- Agencies that want a fully automated legal service product that replaces expert work.
- Businesses that need confirmed government API integrations, OCR, AI review, e-signature, or automatic subscription billing today. These areas are not confirmed in the current repository audit.
