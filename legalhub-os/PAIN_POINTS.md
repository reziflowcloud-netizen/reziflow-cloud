# Pain Points - LegalHub CRM

Last updated: 2026-07-08

Status: marketing foundation v1 based on `PROJECT_BIBLE.md` and `PRODUCT_MAP.md`.

## Overview

LegalHub CRM should speak to the operational pain of legalization agencies that are trying to manage a complex service business with tools that were not built for the workflow.

The repeated pain pattern:

1. Leads arrive from many places.
2. Client data becomes complex quickly.
3. Cases require documents, dates, comments, payments, and status updates.
4. Responsibility becomes unclear as the team grows.
5. The owner loses visibility.
6. Scaling creates more chaos unless the process becomes structured.

## Operational Chaos

### Pain

- The agency runs from WhatsApp, spreadsheets, folders, calendars, and memory.
- Every employee has their own way of tracking cases.
- The owner has to ask for updates instead of seeing the state of work.
- Information is available somewhere, but not in one reliable place.
- New employees cannot easily understand the process.

### What It Sounds Like

- "I know we have this document, but I do not know where."
- "Who is responsible for this client?"
- "Can you send me the latest status again?"
- "Which cases are stuck?"
- "Where is the spreadsheet with the latest version?"

### LegalHub Angle

LegalHub CRM centralizes operational work around leads, clients, cases, tasks, documents, payments, and responsible people.

Safe phrasing:

- "Replace scattered operational tracking with one CRM workspace."
- "Give the team one place to manage legalization agency work."
- "See the workflow from incoming request to active case and follow-up."

## Client Communication

### Pain

- WhatsApp is fast but becomes impossible to control at scale.
- Follow-ups depend on memory or personal chat lists.
- Contact history is hard to reconstruct.
- Clients ask for updates, but the latest status may be hidden in a conversation.
- Leads from forms, social channels, messengers, Telegram, and spreadsheets can be missed.

### What It Sounds Like

- "Did anyone call this lead back?"
- "When was the last contact?"
- "What did we promise the client?"
- "This message was in someone's private chat."
- "The client says they sent it, but nobody can find it."

### LegalHub Angle

LegalHub CRM supports lead records with statuses, sources, responsible people, contact history, messages, reminders, and conversion into client/case records.

Safe phrasing:

- "Help prevent missed follow-ups."
- "Track lead status, source, next contact, and responsible person."
- "Move from chat-based coordination to structured client and case records."

Needs clarification:

- Which messaging integrations can be promoted publicly without setup caveats.

## Documents

### Pain

- Documents are scattered across chats, folders, local computers, and drives.
- Employees do not always know which document version is current.
- Case files are hard to review when documents are disconnected from the case record.
- Generated documents and uploaded files are not always linked to client/case context.
- Document search becomes harder as the agency grows.

### What It Sounds Like

- "Where is the passport scan?"
- "Which power of attorney did we use?"
- "Was this document updated?"
- "Is this attached to the right case?"
- "Who uploaded the latest file?"

### LegalHub Angle

LegalHub CRM supports case document uploads, document comments, DOCX generation from templates, and case-related document storage.

Safe phrasing:

- "Keep case documents connected to the case record."
- "Use document templates to generate selected case documents."
- "Store uploaded case documents in the CRM workflow."

Needs clarification:

- Final list of approved built-in templates.
- Legal review status of templates.
- Approved security/privacy claims for document storage.

## Deadlines

### Pain

- Legalization cases involve many dates and follow-ups.
- Some deadlines live in calendars, some in spreadsheets, some in employee memory.
- Missed dates create client stress and operational risk.
- Payment installment reminders may not be connected to cases.
- Managers do not always see upcoming work in time.

### What It Sounds Like

- "When is the legal stay deadline?"
- "Did we create a reminder for this date?"
- "Which tasks are due this week?"
- "Who is following up?"
- "Why did we notice this so late?"

### LegalHub Angle

LegalHub CRM includes tasks, reminders, calendar views, dashboard upcoming events, lead next-contact reminders, case dates, custom dates, and payment-plan related tasks.

Safe phrasing:

- "Track upcoming tasks and case-related dates."
- "Create reminders connected to clients and cases."
- "Give managers visibility into upcoming work."

Avoid:

- "Never miss a deadline."
- "Guaranteed legal compliance."

## Team Management

### Pain

- It is unclear who owns each lead or case.
- Employees may duplicate work or assume someone else is responsible.
- Managers cannot easily check workload or stalled cases.
- Access needs differ between admins, employees, and restricted users.
- Business responsibility and login permissions are often mixed together in informal systems.

### What It Sounds Like

- "Who is assigned to this case?"
- "Why did two people contact the same client?"
- "Can this employee see only their cases?"
- "Who should update this status?"
- "Where do we manage team roles?"

### LegalHub Angle

LegalHub CRM supports user roles, restricted access, employee/responsible-person records, lead and case assignment, tasks, and management settings.

Safe phrasing:

- "Clarify who is responsible for each lead and case."
- "Manage users, employees, and restricted access from the CRM."
- "Give the team shared ownership rules instead of informal coordination."

## Analytics

### Pain

- Owners do not have a live view of income, debt, clients, active cases, and upcoming work.
- Reports are built manually from spreadsheets.
- Debt becomes visible too late.
- There is no simple way to see new cases and clients over time.
- Decisions are made from partial information.

### What It Sounds Like

- "How much debt is open?"
- "How many active cases do we have?"
- "How many new clients came this month?"
- "Which cases have payment issues?"
- "Do we have enough capacity?"

### LegalHub Angle

LegalHub CRM includes dashboard indicators for income, debt, clients, total cases, active cases, upcoming events, recent cases, and report subpages for income, debt, new cases, and new clients.

Safe phrasing:

- "See key operational and financial signals in the dashboard."
- "Track debt, income, new clients, and active cases from CRM data."
- "Reduce manual reporting from scattered spreadsheets."

Needs clarification:

- Exact customer-facing KPI definitions and which screenshots are approved.

## Sales And Leads

### Pain

- Leads arrive from forms, ads, messengers, Telegram, Google Sheets-style workflows, imports, and manual referrals.
- Hot leads cool down because nobody follows up quickly enough.
- Lead sources and statuses are inconsistent.
- Sales work is mixed with active case work.
- It is hard to know which leads should become clients and cases.

### What It Sounds Like

- "Did this lead come from Facebook, Instagram, referral, or the website?"
- "Who should call this person?"
- "Which leads are overdue?"
- "Which leads have no next contact?"
- "Can we import these old spreadsheet leads?"

### LegalHub Angle

LegalHub CRM supports lead list and board views, statuses, sources, filters, reminders, responsible people, bulk actions, import, external intake paths, and lead conversion to client/case.

Safe phrasing:

- "Organize incoming requests before they become lost opportunities."
- "Track lead source, status, next contact, and responsibility."
- "Convert qualified leads into clients and cases."

Needs clarification:

- Which lead channels are officially supported in production marketing.

## Scaling Pain

### Pain

- The agency can manage chaos with two people, but not with ten.
- Manual spreadsheets become a bottleneck.
- The owner becomes the central router for every decision.
- Adding employees increases coordination problems.
- Custom agency processes need structure without becoming rigid.

### LegalHub Angle

LegalHub CRM supports configurable statuses, services, sources, fields, sections, templates, users, employees, roles, restricted access, dashboards, import/export, and core agency workflows.

Safe phrasing:

- "Build a more repeatable operating system for your agency."
- "Move from owner-dependent coordination to a shared CRM workflow."
- "Configure the CRM around your agency's services and statuses."
