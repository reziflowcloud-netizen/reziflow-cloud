# QA Review - Instagram Launch Batch 001

Status: strict editorial review completed on 2026-07-08.

Reviewer stance: launch content should make a migration agency owner think, "Yes, this is exactly our chaos." Product language should support that recognition, not replace it.

## Executive Verdict

The batch has a strong strategic base, but the first version was too polite in several places. It explained LegalHub CRM clearly, but too often used broad phrases such as "structured workspace", "centralizes", and "workflow" before showing the real operational mess.

The strongest launch angle is not "LegalHub is a CRM." The strongest angle is:

> Your agency already knows the pain: leads without follow-up, documents in chats, cases with no visible owner, deadlines in memory, unpaid balances found too late, and the owner acting as the control center.

Recommended editorial direction:

- Lead with one concrete agency problem per asset.
- Show one LegalHub CRM screen as the answer.
- Use demo/audit/early-access CTAs, not generic "learn more" CTAs.
- Avoid unconfirmed claims about integrations, security, legal template review, automated billing, or guaranteed outcomes.

## 4. Where There Was Too Much CRM Marketing Language

The batch overused:

- "structured workspace";
- "centralizes";
- "workflow";
- "one shared system";
- "operational visibility";
- "case-management workflow";
- "built around the workflow".

These are safe, but they are not sharp enough for Instagram hooks.

Replace them with agency language:

- "Who owns this case?"
- "What is due this week?"
- "Where is the latest document?"
- "Did anyone call this lead back?"
- "What is unpaid?"
- "Which cases are stuck?"
- "What did we promise the client?"
- "How many leads have no next contact?"

Use "CRM workflow" only after the pain is clear.

## 5. Where Client Pain Was Missing

The first version sometimes named categories instead of pain:

- "leads, clients, cases, documents, deadlines, payments";
- "dashboard visibility";
- "workflow";
- "CRM navigation".

The rewrite adds moments the buyer recognizes:

- owner asks the same employee questions every week;
- lead has no owner and no next contact;
- client asks for status and the team searches chat;
- spreadsheet says "in progress" but not what happens next;
- payment debt appears only during manual reporting;
- documents exist, but nobody knows the latest version;
- deadline is in a person's memory, not in a shared task.

## 6. Where LegalHub CRM Should Be Shown Better

Use tighter, screen-specific product demos:

| Pain | CRM screen to show |
| --- | --- |
| Lead follow-up | Lead list filters, lead detail, source/status/responsible person/next contact, conversion action |
| Owner visibility | Dashboard cards, upcoming events, recent cases, cases list with responsible person, debt/income report if approved |
| Case context | Case detail with service, status, responsible person, dates, comments, documents, payments, tasks |
| Documents | Case documents section, upload/list, preview/download, comment/update context, templates only with caveat |
| Deadlines | Tasks board, task detail, calendar, dashboard upcoming events |
| Spreadsheet migration | CSV import/export or lead import only if approved demo data is ready; avoid "automatic migration" |

Do not show:

- real client data;
- integration tokens or setup secrets;
- unapproved integration screens;
- security/GDPR wording;
- pricing/billing claims;
- legal-template claims beyond "DOCX generation from templates".

## 9. Ideas To Delete Or Rewrite

Rewrite:

- "This week we are showing how LegalHub CRM organizes the agency workflow."
  - Too launch-oriented. Replace with a direct diagnostic: "How many active cases can your team explain without opening three tools?"
- "Generic CRM starts with sales. LegalHub starts with legalization operations."
  - Useful, but too abstract. Replace with: "A client is not just a contact card. A case is not just a deal."
- "Share the carousel with your operations manager."
  - Weak CTA. Replace with demo/audit or "reply with the part your team cannot see today."
- "Ask us anything about moving from Excel to CRM."
  - Keep, but frame around risk: "What would make your team afraid to leave Excel?"

Remove from public copy unless clarified:

- any implied automatic WhatsApp import;
- any implied native Google Sheets integration;
- any "legally reviewed templates" claim;
- any "GDPR-compliant" claim;
- any "never miss" or "never lose" guarantee.

## Final Editorial Rules For Batch 001

- Start every asset with a concrete broken moment.
- Use one main pain per asset.
- Show one or two CRM screens, not a full product tour.
- Keep captions diagnostic and specific.
- Make the CTA lead to demo request, workflow audit, audit call, or early access.
- Keep all unsupported claims marked as `Needs clarification`.
