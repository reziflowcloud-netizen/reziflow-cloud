# Stories Plan - 14 Days

Status: QA-revised Instagram Stories plan for launch.

Primary use: collect replies, qualify pain, answer objections, and move warm viewers toward a demo, audit call, or early access.

Editorial rule after QA:

- Ask concrete agency questions.
- Use polls to identify pain: leads, documents, deadlines, payments, owner visibility.
- Use product screens only when they answer the pain.
- Push warm viewers to demo/audit/early access.

Needs clarification before publishing:

- Final booking link.
- Whether story replies and DM keywords are handled manually or automated.
- Approved demo data and screenshots.
- Approved primary CTA.
- Whether early access should be offered publicly.

## Best Lead-Generation Story Days

Use these as priority if the team cannot publish all 14 days:

Frame 2: "Chat? Spreadsheet? Folder? Calendar? Employee message?"

Frame 3: "If the answer is 3+, your agency is not too small for CRM. It is too busy for scattered tracking."

### Afternoon Story

Show a simple map:

Lead -> Client -> Case -> Documents -> Dates -> Tasks -> Payments -> Responsible person.

### Evening Story
