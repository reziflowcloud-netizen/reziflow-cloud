# Publishing Plan - First 14 Days

Status: QA-revised launch publishing order.

Primary campaign narrative:

LegalHub CRM helps legalization agencies in Poland move from Excel, WhatsApp, scattered folders, memory, and owner-dependent coordination into a clearer CRM workflow for leads, clients, cases, documents, deadlines, payments, tasks, and responsible people.

Editorial change after QA:

- Do not open the launch with a broad "we are a CRM" statement.
- Open with the pains the owner already feels: spreadsheet limits, missed follow-up, owner questions, incomplete case status, documents in chats, debt visibility, and deadlines in memory.
- Use Reel 01 as a support/recap asset, not the first post.

Needs clarification before launch:

- Final CTA and booking link.
- Approved demo screenshots and demo data.
- Final DM/comment handling process.
- Approved profile link-in-bio destination.
- Whether early access should be offered publicly.

## Recommended Daily Rhythm

Morning:

- Publish the primary Reel or carousel.
- Add 1-2 Stories that ask a concrete diagnostic question.

Afternoon:

- Add one product or workflow Story.
- Use poll/question stickers to collect replies.

Evening:

- Share a short recap Story and one CTA.
- Reply manually to qualified DMs if automation is not approved.

Needs clarification: exact posting times should be based on account analytics after the first week.

## Production Guardrails

- Use demo data only.
- Blur or replace all real personal data.
- Do not show integration tokens, webhook keys, or unapproved provider screens.
- Do not claim automatic migration, automatic WhatsApp import, official native Google Sheets integration, automated billing, legal template review, GDPR compliance, or guaranteed outcomes.
- Use "helps track", "supports", "can show", and "workflow demo" language.
