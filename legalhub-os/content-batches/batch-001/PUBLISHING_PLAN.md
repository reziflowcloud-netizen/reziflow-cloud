## Recommended Daily Rhythm

Morning:

- Publish the primary Reel or carousel between the audience's planning windows.
- Add 1-2 Stories introducing the day's pain.

Afternoon:

- Add one product or workflow Story.
- Use poll/question stickers to collect replies.

Evening:

- Share a recap Story and soft CTA.
- Reply manually to qualified DMs if automation is not approved.

Needs clarification: exact posting times should be based on account analytics after the first week.
