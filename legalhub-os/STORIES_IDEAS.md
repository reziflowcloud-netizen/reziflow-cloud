# Stories Ideas - LegalHub CRM

Last updated: 2026-07-08

Status: 42 Instagram Stories ideas for interaction, trust, product education, and lead generation.

## Usage Notes

Stories should be used to create replies, objections, and workflow conversations. They are ideal for diagnosing the prospect's current process before inviting them to a demo or workflow audit.

Needs clarification before production:

- Final primary CTA.
- Whether story replies and DM keywords are handled manually or through automation.
- Approved demo data and screenshots.
- Approved claims for integrations, pricing, and security/privacy.
