# Objections - LegalHub CRM

Last updated: 2026-07-08

Status: marketing foundation v1 based on `PROJECT_BIBLE.md` and `PRODUCT_MAP.md`.

## Objection-Handling Principles

Use objection handling to reduce risk, not to pressure the buyer.

Good answers should:

- acknowledge the current workflow;
- connect the objection to a real operational pain;
- explain the product in plain terms;
- avoid absolute promises;
- ask a useful follow-up question.

## "We Already Have Excel"

### Response

Excel is useful for lists, but legalization agencies need more than rows and columns. Once you manage leads, clients, cases, deadlines, documents, payments, tasks, and responsible people, the spreadsheet becomes an operating risk.

LegalHub CRM helps turn that work into structured records: leads can become clients and cases, cases can hold documents, payments, comments, dates, tasks, and status history, and managers can see operational signals from CRM data.

### Proof Points To Use

- CSV import and mapping.
- Lead import with duplicate handling.
- Client, case, lead, payment, and all-data exports.
- Dashboard and report pages.
- Lead-to-client-to-case workflow.

### Follow-Up Question

Which spreadsheet is hardest to keep accurate today: leads, clients, active cases, payments, or deadlines?

## "It Is Too Early For Us To Use CRM"

### Response

It may be too early for a complex enterprise system, but it is usually not too early to create a clean process. Legalization work becomes complex quickly because every client can involve documents, dates, follow-ups, payments, and status changes.

Starting earlier helps the agency build good habits before the workflow becomes difficult to clean up.

### Proof Points To Use

- Free plan exists in code with defined usage limits. Needs clarification: final public pricing approval.
- Core workflow supports small teams as well as growing agencies.
- Configurable statuses, services, sources, and fields.

### Follow-Up Question

At what point would your current system become difficult: more leads, more clients, more employees, or more active cases?

## "We Do Not Have Many Clients"

### Response

CRM value is not only about the number of clients. It is also about the number of tasks, documents, deadlines, payments, and follow-ups connected to each client.

Even a small client base can create operational risk if important dates live in memory, documents are scattered, or only one person knows the case status.

### Proof Points To Use

- Client and case records.
- Tasks, reminders, and calendar.
- Case documents and comments.
- Payment/debt tracking.
- Dashboard upcoming events.

### Follow-Up Question

How many active cases can your team manage before the owner has to check everything manually?

## "CRM Is Hard To Implement"

### Response

Generic CRMs can be hard because you have to build the workflow yourself. LegalHub CRM starts from the workflow of legalization agencies: requests, leads, clients, cases, documents, deadlines, payments, and responsible people.

The goal is not to configure everything on day one. The practical starting point is to map your current lead and case workflow, import or enter the first records, define statuses, and make sure the team knows where to work.

### Proof Points To Use

- Organization settings for services, statuses, lead sources, users, employees, custom fields, and templates.
- Import/export flows.
- Quick-start/onboarding UI exists for admins/owners.

### Follow-Up Question

What would make implementation feel easier for your agency: spreadsheet import, onboarding call, demo setup, or workflow audit?

### Needs Clarification

- Approved onboarding promise.
- Whether guided onboarding is included by plan.

## "Employees Will Not Use It"

### Response

Employees usually reject CRM when it feels like extra reporting. Adoption improves when the CRM helps them do daily work: see assigned cases, find documents, track tasks, add comments, check dates, and know what needs attention next.

LegalHub CRM should be positioned as a shared workspace for case work, not just a monitoring tool for management.

### Proof Points To Use

- Assigned users and responsible employees.
- Restricted employee access concept.
- Tasks, reminders, calendar.
- Lead and case views.
- Documents, comments, and status history.

### Follow-Up Question

What do employees complain about most today: searching for information, remembering deadlines, unclear responsibility, or duplicate work?

## "How Are You Better Than A Normal CRM?"

### Response

A normal CRM usually starts with a sales pipeline. LegalHub CRM starts with the legalization agency workflow.

The difference is the operating model: your team needs to manage not only leads, but also complex client profiles, active cases, immigration-related data, documents, dates, payments, comments, statuses, and responsible people.

Generic CRMs can sometimes be customized for this, but LegalHub CRM is built around these objects from the start.

### Proof Points To Use

- Legalization-specific client and case fields.
- Lead conversion into client and optional case.
- Case status history, comments, documents, dates, payments, and tasks.
- Configurable services, statuses, sources, fields, and templates.
- Dashboard/reporting around agency operations.

### Follow-Up Question

Which part of your workflow does a normal CRM fail to represent: client data, case status, documents, deadlines, payments, or team responsibility?

## "Why Specifically For Migration / Legalization Business?"

### Response

Migration and legalization work has a different operational structure from ordinary sales. A client is not just a contact, and a case is not just a deal.

Each case can involve detailed personal data, passport and stay information, service type, status history, documents, important dates, payments, comments, and responsible team members. LegalHub CRM is designed around that pattern.

### Proof Points To Use

- Product positioning and repository data model focus on legalization companies in Poland.
- Client records include immigration, passport, residence, address, travel, family, and custom data areas.
- Case records include legalization-related dates, status, documents, payments, comments, and history.

### Follow-Up Question

Which legalization-specific information is hardest to manage in your current system?

## "We Use WhatsApp For Everything"

### Response

WhatsApp is good for conversation, but it is not a reliable operating system for agency work. It is hard to see status, ownership, deadlines, documents, payments, and history across all clients and cases.

LegalHub CRM gives the team a structured place to manage the work that happens around the conversation.

### Proof Points To Use

- Lead, client, and case records.
- Contact history and lead messages.
- Tasks, reminders, calendar, dashboard upcoming events.
- Documents and comments connected to cases.

### Follow-Up Question

What usually gets lost in WhatsApp today: documents, follow-ups, deadlines, decisions, or responsibility?

### Needs Clarification

- Do not promise automatic WhatsApp import or official WhatsApp integration unless confirmed.

## "Our Cases Are Too Specific"

### Response

That is exactly why a generic contact database is not enough. LegalHub CRM includes structured areas for client and case data, but also supports configurable statuses, services, lead sources, case options, custom sections, and custom fields.

The goal is to give the agency structure without forcing every case into a rigid generic pipeline.

### Proof Points To Use

- Custom fields and custom sections.
- Configurable statuses, services, lead sources, case options.
- Detailed client and case data model.

### Follow-Up Question

Which fields or statuses would you need to see in your first CRM setup?

## "What About Data Security And Privacy?"

### Response

This is a valid question, especially for agencies handling personal and immigration-related data.

The product has public privacy/data-deletion pages and document storage flows, but marketing should only use approved security and compliance language after legal/product review.

### Safe Answer

We can explain where data is stored, how access roles work, and what privacy documents are available. For formal compliance claims, we should provide approved documentation.

### Proof Points To Use Carefully

- Public privacy and data-deletion pages exist.
- User roles and restricted access exist.
- Cloudinary storage and optional Dropbox copy exist for documents.

### Needs Clarification

- Approved GDPR/security/privacy wording.
- Backup, retention, deletion, and storage-region policies.

## "Which Integrations Do You Support?"

### Response

LegalHub CRM includes webhook-based lead intake and integration paths for external sources, but each integration depends on setup and production readiness.

The safest public message is that LegalHub CRM can connect external lead sources through supported setup flows and webhooks. Specific provider claims should be confirmed before campaigns.

### Proof Points To Use Carefully

- Generic lead webhook.
- Weblium-friendly webhook URL pattern.
- Google Sheets Apps Script sample forwarding rows to the CRM webhook.
- Meta/Facebook/Instagram integration paths.
- Telegram webhook path.
- Incoming webhook logs.

### Needs Clarification

- Production readiness of Meta/Facebook/Instagram, Telegram, Google Sheets, Dropbox.
- Meta App Review / Advanced Access status.
- Whether Google Sheets should be described as Apps Script/webhook rather than native integration.
