# Reels Ideas - LegalHub CRM

Last updated: 2026-07-08

Status: 60 Reels ideas grounded in confirmed LegalHub CRM workflows.

## Usage Notes

Each Reel should start from a specific agency pain, then show the CRM workflow as the practical response. Avoid generic "our CRM is great" framing.

Needs clarification before filming:

- Approved demo data and screenshots.
- Final CTA and booking path.
- Which integration screens can be shown publicly.
- Approved security/privacy and pricing language.
